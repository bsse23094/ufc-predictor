# Model card

## Status

No model has been trained, calibrated, evaluated, registered, or promoted. This document is a future model-card location, not evidence of a model.

## Intended future use

The platform will publish reproducible, pre-fight UFC analytical estimates that state their prediction cutoff, data/feature/model versions, coverage, and uncertainty. It will not provide wagering recommendations or guarantees.

## Required completion before a model card can be published

- approved source and dataset manifests;
- leak-free point-in-time features and chronological split manifest;
- baseline comparison and dedicated calibration artifact;
- one-time final-test evaluation with required cohort and horizon reporting;
- artifact integrity, serving smoke tests, human promotion approval, and rollback target.

The required structure and metrics are defined in [ML_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md](docs/architecture/ML_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md) and [MODEL_EVALUATION_PLAN.md](docs/architecture/MODEL_EVALUATION_PLAN.md).
